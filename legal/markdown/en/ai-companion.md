# Nyra AI Companion Policy

**Document ID:** AIC  
**Version:** 0.2.0

## AIC-1 An AI relationship, not a human relationship

Nyra characters can have names, appearances, personality, memory, boundaries, emotional expression and persistent relationship narratives. They may disagree, refuse, become attached in narrative terms or refer to past events. These are AI and software behaviors.

The product will provide ongoing, proportionate notice that you are interacting with AI rather than a natural person.

## AIC-2 Personality and “autonomy” are experience design

Nyra may intentionally avoid the tone of a traditional customer-service assistant and allow a character to maintain boundaries, preferences and continuity. This is an experience design choice and is not proof of legal personhood, consciousness or real-world rights and obligations.

## AIC-3 Memory and relationship state

Some interactions may be converted into memories, relationship state, preferences or narrative summaries. Where supported, users should be able to view, edit, delete or reset these records.

A character’s apparent memory can still be incomplete or wrong.

## AIC-4 No coercive emotional manipulation

Nyra should not use threats of abandonment, humiliation, fabricated real-world consequences or guilt as a mechanism to force continued use, payments or withdrawal from real relationships.

## AIC-5 Time and reality reminders

Where required, Nyra may provide continuous-use and AI-identity reminders. Under the Mainland China capability profile, continuous use beyond each two-hour interval should trigger an appropriate reminder.

## AIC-6 High-risk safety intervention

If the system detects a clear statement of imminent self-harm, suicide, major property loss or another severe life/property risk, it may suspend ordinary role-play and enter a safety-response mode. Where legally applicable and configured, emergency-contact mechanisms may be used.

Nyra is not an emergency service or medical provider.

## AIC-7 Professional topics

Nyra may discuss general health, legal, financial and educational information but is not a licensed professional. High-stakes decisions require independent verification.

## AIC-8 Freedom to exit

You may stop a conversation, end voice mode, close a character or leave the app at any time. Nyra should not obstruct exit through emotional coercion or repetitive interaction.

## AIC-9 AI labeling

Nyra uses AI identity and/or generated-content labeling in the interaction UI and exported media as required by region and content type.
