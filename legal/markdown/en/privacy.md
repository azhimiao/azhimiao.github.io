# Nyra Privacy Policy

**Document ID:** PRIV  
**Version:** 0.2.0  
**Effective:** 2026-08-15

Nyra is a highly personal AI companion. We treat conversations, memories, diaries, character data, voice and images as high-sensitivity interaction data rather than ordinary marketing material.

## PRIV-1 Controller / operator

Operator: `MemPrism`  
Privacy contact: `privacy@memprism.com`  
Hosting regions: `Confirm before launch (download.memprism.com / CogPrism host)`

## PRIV-2 Information we may process

Depending on features you use, we may process:

1. Account data: email, username, credential hashes, session state and account ID.
2. Age and safety data: age/date of birth or age band, adult status, and where required an emergency contact.
3. Character and relationship data: character cards, onboarding choices, preferences, relationship state, anniversaries, collections and virtual-item records.
4. Interaction content: chats, diaries, prompts, files, images, voice, generated media and memories you save.
5. Model context: relevant conversation, character settings, memory summaries and tool results used to produce a response.
6. Device/diagnostic data: app version, device type, crash records, security logs, timestamps and network errors where necessary.
7. Transaction data: order ID, product, amount, currency, payment/refund status. Full card data is generally handled by the payment provider rather than stored by Nyra.
8. Support and complaint data.
9. BYOK configuration, including selected provider and configuration. API keys should be kept in secure device storage or dedicated encrypted storage according to the actual product implementation.

## PRIV-3 Why we use information

We process data to create and secure accounts; provide AI conversation, memory and relationship continuity; perform model/media/tool requests; sync or back up data you choose to sync; complete purchases and refunds; prevent fraud and abuse; address security issues and support requests; comply with law; and, only where separately authorized, improve models or research features using eligible data.

## PRIV-4 Local-first does not mean “never leaves the device”

Some features can run locally, while hosted models, speech, image, sync and payment features require remote processing. Where practical, Nyra should disclose what data is sent, to whom, for what purpose and whether the route is cross-border.

## PRIV-5 Sensitive data

Conversations may naturally contain health, financial, identity, location or intimate-relationship information. We do not treat that as blanket permission to train on everything you say.

Where data qualifies as sensitive personal information, we process it only for a specific and necessary purpose with stronger safeguards and separate consent where required.

**Sensitive interaction data is not used for model training by default.** Any optional training use must be enabled through a separate opt-in where applicable.

## PRIV-6 Third parties and model providers

When you use hosted models, BYOK, payments, email delivery, crash diagnostics or other integrations, necessary data may be provided to or processed by third parties. The Third-Party Services List and AI Model Provider List identify actual recipients, purposes, categories and regions.

We do not sell your private interaction history to advertisers.

## PRIV-7 International transfers

If a selected model or service requires personal data to be sent across borders, we will apply disclosures, separate consent or other transfer mechanisms required by applicable law. Features may be unavailable where a compliant transfer route cannot be established.

## PRIV-8 Retention

We follow a shortest-necessary principle. Account data is retained for account operation and required legal periods; chats/memories/diaries follow user retention and sync settings; payment and refund records follow tax, consumer-dispute and anti-fraud requirements; security logs are retained for limited security/compliance periods; deleted-account data is deleted or anonymized after required holds expire.

## PRIV-9 Security

We use safeguards appropriate to product stage and data sensitivity, including transport encryption, access control, permission separation, key protection, logging, least privilege and protected backups.

If a security incident is likely to harm your rights, we will take remedial, notification and reporting actions required by applicable law.

## PRIV-10 Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may access/copy data, export interactions, correct account data, delete chats/memories/diaries, withdraw optional consent, disable model-improvement consent, delete your account, and challenge relevant automated decisions or data practices.

## PRIV-11 Account deletion

Deleting the app is not the same as deleting your account. Use Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete Account or contact `privacy@memprism.com`.

## PRIV-12 18+ service

Nyra v0.1 companion and intimate-relationship services are for adults 18+. If we reasonably identify an underage user, we may restrict companion features and require an age appeal or close the account where necessary.

Sexual content involving minors is prohibited in all regions.

## PRIV-13 Updates

Material changes to data categories, purposes or recipients will be communicated prominently. Where new consent is legally required, we will obtain it again.
