# Nyra Billing, Digital Delivery & Refund Policy

**Document ID:** BILLING  
**Version:** 0.2.0

> **Important purchase notice.** Nyra primarily sells digital access, software, digital features, virtual items and compute/API resources — not a human after-sales support package. Once a digital entitlement has been downloaded, activated, delivered or actually consumed, it is generally non-refundable except where mandatory law, store/channel rules, an express purchase-page commitment or a Nyra billing error requires otherwise. Material restrictions should be conspicuously disclosed before payment.

## BILLING-1 Paid products

Nyra may offer memberships/subscriptions, Billing Credit, one-time digital features/extensions, downloadable software/content, generation quotas, virtual items and third-party-channel packages.

The purchase page defines the primary scope of delivery. Human support, custom development, remote repair, long-term maintenance, guaranteed outcomes or SLA are not part of an order unless expressly listed.

## BILLING-2 Billing Credit

Billing Credit represents prepaid access to digital compute/API/generation resources. It is not cash, a deposit, e-money, a security, crypto, an investment or a general monetary claim against Nyra.

Credits may be consumed based on requests, tokens, calls, images, speech duration or another disclosed unit. Future packages may change if provider prices or costs change; completed orders are not retroactively repriced.

## BILLING-3 When digital delivery occurs

Depending on the product, delivery may occur when a download/license/activation becomes available; a membership is activated; a digital feature is unlocked; credits are consumed; a billable model/API/generation job starts; a virtual item becomes available in inventory; or a third-party channel confirms entitlement delivery.

Consumed and unconsumed portions of divisible resources may be treated separately.

## BILLING-4 Generally non-refundable items

Subject to mandatory law, store/payment-channel rules and express purchase-page commitments:

- actually consumed Billing Credit is non-refundable;
- successfully downloaded, activated or delivered software/digital features are generally non-refundable;
- completed generation jobs that incurred resource cost are not refundable merely because the user dislikes the subjective output;
- used, gifted, consumed or irreversibly changed virtual items are generally non-refundable;
- once a subscription billing period has started, the current period is generally not prorated, although future renewal can be cancelled;
- problems caused by the user's device/network, third-party models, BYOK configuration, third-party bans/pricing, user error or unsupported environments generally do not create a Nyra refund obligation.

AI aesthetics, personality and wording are inherently subjective. A request that was correctly executed and delivered does not become refundable solely because the output was not preferred.

## BILLING-5 Narrow refund/correction cases

Nyra will address duplicate charges, clear Nyra billing errors, payment with total non-delivery, measurable credit deductions caused by a Nyra failure before billable execution, legally/store-mandated refunds and express refund promises on a purchase page.

Where measurable, Nyra may first correct the issue by restoring credit, re-delivering the entitlement or another equivalent remedy unless law requires a cash refund.

## BILLING-6 Downloadable software and statutory cooling-off rights

Where applicable law expressly excludes online-downloaded computer software or similar digital goods from a no-reason return right, that statutory exception applies. Other digital products/services are assessed under applicable law, product nature, pre-purchase confirmation and channel rules.

Where separate confirmation is legally required to exclude a no-reason return, Nyra should obtain a conspicuous pre-payment confirmation rather than relying on hidden text or default consent.

## BILLING-7 Prepayments and discontinuation

If a product legally constitutes a prepaid service, undelivered portions are handled under the purchase terms and mandatory law. Experimental status does not override mandatory rules for unused prepayments.

Free/test/promotional credits may be discontinued without cash conversion. Paid but undelivered entitlements are handled under the order and applicable law.

## BILLING-8 Auto-renewal

Auto-renewal must not be preselected. Price, period and cancellation method are disclosed before purchase, with renewal reminders where required. Cancelling auto-renewal does not terminate a period already paid for before its expiry.

## BILLING-9 Promotional and free credits

Free, promotional, testing, developer or goodwill credits may have expiry dates and feature restrictions and may end with the relevant program. Unless mandatory law requires otherwise, they have no cash redemption value.

## BILLING-10 Chargebacks and payment disputes

Report unknown charges promptly. Fraudulent chargebacks, stolen payment methods or abuse of refund systems may lead to transaction/account restrictions. Good-faith consumer disputes are not punished. Third-party API/model charges should be disputed with the third party that charged them.

## BILLING-11 Support boundary

Buying a digital entitlement does not automatically purchase human after-sales support. Nyra may offer documentation, FAQ, email, community or issue-tracker support on a best-effort basis, but no fixed response time, remote-operation obligation or device-by-device resolution commitment exists unless a paid support plan expressly states one.
