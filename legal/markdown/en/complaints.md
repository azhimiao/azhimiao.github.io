# Nyra Complaints, Appeals & Enforcement

**Document ID:** APPEAL  
**Version:** 0.2.0

## APPEAL-1 What you can report

You may report or appeal account restrictions, content refusals, rights-infringing output, privacy/data concerns, billing/refund issues, underage/safety concerns, and other policy or legal issues.

## APPEAL-2 Channels

In-app: `Settings → Help → Report / Discord @cogprism / QQ 1034044082`  
Email: `support@memprism.com`

## APPEAL-3 Response target

Our target is to acknowledge ordinary cases within **5 business days** and resolve them or provide a status update within **15 business days**. Complex safety, billing, third-party or legally investigated matters may take longer.

## APPEAL-4 Enforcement ladder

Depending on severity, Nyra may apply content refusal, warning, temporary feature restriction, login/payment suspension or account termination.

Where feasible, account-level measures will include a reason category and appeal path unless immediate action is legally required or explanation would create a material safety risk.

## APPEAL-5 Rights-holder reports

If you believe Nyra content violates copyright, publicity, privacy or other rights, provide enough information to identify the content and establish your claim. Disputed content may be restricted while reviewed.

## APPEAL-6 External remedies

Nothing here prevents you from using regulators, consumer organizations, courts, payment providers or app-store remedies available under applicable law.
