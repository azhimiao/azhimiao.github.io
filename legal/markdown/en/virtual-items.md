# Nyra Virtual Items & Nyra Coin Policy

**Document ID:** VIRTUAL  
**Version:** 0.2.0

## VIRTUAL-1 What Nyra Coin is

Nyra Coin / 栖币 is an **in-product gameplay accounting unit** used for relationship gameplay, virtual shop items, gifts and related interaction records.

Under v0.1, Nyra Coin:

- is not fiat currency;
- is not cryptocurrency, electronic money, a deposit, security or investment;
- does not promise appreciation, yield or interest;
- is not redeemable for cash from Nyra;
- does not create a debt claim against the operator;
- may not be used to create a real-money secondary market.

## VIRTUAL-2 Separate from Billing Credit

Nyra Coin and Billing Credit are separate ledgers. Nyra Coin is gameplay economy; Billing Credit is prepaid access to actual digital compute/API resources. They are not mutually convertible by default.

## VIRTUAL-3 Earning and spending

Nyra Coin may be granted through initial allocation, events, relationship gameplay or other in-product mechanics and used for supported virtual items, gifts or gameplay.

In-product “transfer” or “payment” UI represents only an internal virtual ledger change, not a real-world financial transfer or debt.

## VIRTUAL-4 Virtual items

Gifts, skins, expressions, collectibles and decorations generally grant a limited in-Nyra usage license and do not transfer underlying art, code or brand intellectual property.

## VIRTUAL-5 Future changes

If Nyra later allows real-money purchase of Nyra Coin or changes it into a redeemable/prepaid instrument, this policy must be revised before launch of that feature.
