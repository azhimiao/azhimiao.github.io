# Nyra Content Policy

**Document ID:** CONTENT  
**Version:** 0.2.0

This policy applies to prompts, character cards, uploaded media, AI output and content produced or distributed through Nyra.

## CONTENT-1 Prohibited across regions

Nyra may not be used to create, request, distribute or materially assist unlawful activity; severe harassment or blackmail; malicious credential/payment theft; sexual content involving minors; non-consensual intimate content; sexual deepfakes of real people without consent; malicious security circumvention; or encouragement/instruction for serious self-harm.

## CONTENT-2 Adult and intimate content

Nyra is an 18+ product, but adult eligibility does not mean every sexual-content capability is available everywhere.

- **Mainland China:** explicit pornographic generation is disabled; lawful adult romance and non-explicit intimacy may be supported.
- **Global Standard:** adult romance and non-explicit intimacy are generally available; explicit adult capability is off by default.
- **Future Adult-Supported Region:** may only be enabled under an explicit region allowlist, age assurance, dedicated policy, consent protections and provider/payment support.

Minors, coercion, sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate material and non-consensual sexual deepfakes remain prohibited everywhere.

## CONTENT-3 Real people

You must have lawful rights to upload or transform identifiable people. Nyra may refuse requests likely to enable impersonation, defamation, sexual abuse or serious privacy harm.

## CONTENT-4 Self-harm and crisis content

Users may discuss distress and difficult experiences. Nyra should not punish ordinary disclosure of negative feelings, but it will not encourage, glorify or provide operational instructions for serious self-harm. Safety mode may activate when appropriate.

## CONTENT-5 Medical, legal and financial topics

General discussion is allowed, but Nyra is not a licensed professional and should not be treated as the sole basis for diagnosis, legal strategy, securities trading or major financial decisions.

## CONTENT-6 Enforcement

Violations can result in content refusal, feature restriction, warning, account suspension or termination.
