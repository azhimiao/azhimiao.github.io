# Nyra Regional Availability Policy

**Document ID:** REGION  
**Version:** 0.2.0

## REGION-1 Why capabilities vary

Nyra uses one brand and core product, but law, age requirements, model providers, payment channels and content rules vary by jurisdiction. Some models, media features, intimate content, payment methods or cloud functions may therefore be region-limited.

## REGION-2 Server-side capability policy

Nyra may use account region, store region, payment region, network signals and other reasonable information to assign a service profile. We avoid collecting precise location by default unless a specific feature genuinely requires it.

Users must not falsify identity or region data to evade legally required controls.

## REGION-3 Mainland China

The Mainland China profile follows applicable generative-AI, anthropomorphic-interaction, personal-information, content-safety and AI-labeling requirements. Defaults include 18+ companion use, AI identity notice, no explicit pornographic generation, continuous-use/dependency reminders, interaction-data copy/delete controls, separate consent for eligible sensitive-interaction training, and applicable cross-border controls for overseas models.

## REGION-4 Global Standard

The standard international profile supports adult AI companionship, relationship features, creative tools and lawful non-explicit intimacy, subject to provider policy.

## REGION-5 Future Adult-Supported Region

v0.1 does not enable a default explicit-adult region. Any future activation requires a jurisdiction allowlist, meaningful age assurance, dedicated adult policy, consent/real-person protections, provider and payment support, and a separate capability flag.

## REGION-6 Unavailable regions

We may restrict all or part of the service where prohibited by law, sanctions, app-store rules, payment restrictions or provider terms. Material regional limits should be shown before purchase where practical.
