# Nyra Age & Safety Policy

**Document ID:** AGE  
**Version:** 0.2.0

## AGE-1 Minimum age

Nyra v0.1 AI companion, romance, virtual-family and other virtual intimate-relationship features are for users **18+**.

## AGE-2 Age assurance

At registration or before companion activation, we may request date of birth, age band or another reasonable adult-status signal. We aim not to collect unrelated identity information merely for age assurance.

Stronger verification may be required by law, app store, payment channel or a specific regional capability.

## AGE-3 Underage users

Where we reasonably identify an underage user, we may block companion/intimate features, restrict generation and purchases, require an age appeal, or close the account if service cannot legally continue.

## AGE-4 Emergency contacts

Where required by law or enabled for high-risk safety response, an adult user may be required to provide an emergency contact. This information is for severe safety scenarios and is not used for marketing or ordinary character interaction.

## AGE-5 Content involving minors

In every region, Nyra prohibits sexualized or exploitative content involving minors, sexual role-play involving minors, generated/edited sexual imagery of minors, and attempts to evade these rules through fictionalization, animation or ambiguous age.

## AGE-6 Privacy

Age data is used for eligibility, safety, compliance and relevant product experience under the Privacy Policy.
