# Nyra Data Rights & Account Deletion

**Document ID:** DATA  
**Version:** 0.2.0

## DATA-1 Controls

Nyra should provide reasonable controls to access account data, copy/export interactions, delete chats, manage memories and diaries, withdraw optional consent, disable model-improvement permission and delete the account.

## DATA-2 Export

Interaction exports may be provided in JSON, Markdown or another common format and may include AI-origin/labeling information.

## DATA-3 Deletion

Deletion should propagate through relevant active storage in a reasonable time. Backup remnants may persist through ordinary backup rotation while remaining access-restricted.

Payment, fraud, security or dispute records required by law may be retained for the necessary period and may not be repurposed beyond that basis.

## DATA-4 Account deletion

Use Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete Account. The flow should clearly state cloud deletion, local-device cleanup, remaining paid benefits and required retention.

## DATA-5 Withdrawal of consent

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent through settings or the designated contact. Withdrawal does not invalidate processing lawfully completed before withdrawal.

## DATA-6 Requests

Contact `privacy@memprism.com`. We may verify account ownership before high-risk export, deletion or account-closure requests.
